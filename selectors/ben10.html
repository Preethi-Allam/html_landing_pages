Ben Tennyson is a 10-year-old boy who discovers a mysterious alien device called the Omnitrix while on a summer road trip with his cousin Gwen and grandfather Max.
The Omnitrix attaches itself to Ben's wrist and allows him to transform into different alien species.
So instead of having just one superpower, Ben potentially has an entire collection of alien abilities.

What is OMNITRIX

The Omnitrix is the most important object in the series. It looks like a watch, but it's actually an extremely advanced alien device. Ben can select an alien and transform into it.

Some famous original aliens include

  1. Heatblast: Fire and heat manipulation
  2. Four Arms: Incredible strength
  3. XLR8: Super speed
  4. Diamondhead: Crystal-based body and weapons
  5. Cannonbolt: Rolls into a powerful armored ball
  6. Upgrade: Merges with and controls technology
  7. Ghostfreak: Intangibility and possession-like abilities
  8. Wildmutt: Enhanced senses and physical abilities
  9. Stinkfly: Flight and sticky/projectile attacks
  10. Grey Matter: Extremely high intelligence
