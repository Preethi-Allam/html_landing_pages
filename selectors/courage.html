COURAGE
The Cowardly Dog

Courage the Cowardly Dog is basically about a scared little dog protecting the people he loves from bizarre and supernatural threats.
Courage is a small pink dog who lives with an elderly couple, Muriel and Eustace Bagge, in a farmhouse located in the middle of Nowhere, Kansas.
Courage is terrified of almost everything.
Even though Courage is scared, he almost always fights to protect Muriel.
He's nervous, paranoid and easily frightened. But when Muriel is in danger, Courage doesn't run away permanently. He eventually goes: "I have to save Muriel." And then somehow finds a way to defeat whatever terrifying creature has appeared.

Some legendary episodes

  1. King Ramses' Curse

    One of the most famous. Courage discovers an ancient Egyptian artifact connected to King Ramses. Then a terrifying supernatural figure appears. And there's that unforgettable: "Return the slab." The episode is genuinely creepy even today.

  2. The Mask

    This is one of the darker episodes. Courage encounters a mysterious masked girl named Kitty. The story explores friendship, abuse, fear and escaping a toxic relationship. It's surprisingly mature for a cartoon.

  3. Freaky Fred

    Courage encounters Fred, Muriel's nephew. Fred has an extremely creepy obsession with shaving people's hair. The episode uses psychological horror rather than a traditional monster.

  4. Perfect

    This is another surprisingly deep episode. Courage is constantly told that he's not good enough. Eventually, the episode becomes a story about self-worth and acceptance.

Finally this cartoon tells about:
"A horror-comedy cartoon about a terrified dog living in the middle of nowhere, where something insane happens almost every day."
