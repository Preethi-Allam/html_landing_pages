Absolutely. Doraemon is one of the most famous Japanese cartoon/anime series, especially popular across India and many Asian countries.
Doraemon is a robotic cat from the 22nd century who travels back in time to help a young boy named Nobita Nobi.

Characters

  1. Doraemon: Robotic cat from the future; Nobita's helper and best friend
  2. Nobita: The main human character; frequently gets into trouble
  3. Dorami: She is the Doraemon's younger sister
  4. Shizuka: Kind, intelligent and caring; Nobita likes her
  5. Gian: Strong, loud and sometimes aggressive, but can be loyal
  6. Suneo: Wealthy, boastful and clever; often shows off

A typical Doraemon episode works something like:
Nobita has a problem → Nobita asks Doraemon for a gadget → Doraemon gives him one → Nobita becomes overconfident → he abuses the gadget → everything goes wrong → Doraemon has to fix the situation.

The famous gadgets

  1. Anywhere Door — lets you travel instantly to another location.
  2. Take-copter — a small propeller that lets you fly.
  3. Time Machine — allows travel through time.
  4. Small Light / Big Light — makes objects smaller or larger
  5. Time Furoshiki — can change an object's age/state through time.
  6. Translation Tool — helps characters understand other languages.

The interesting part is that Nobita usually uses these gadgets to solve a simple problem, but then misuses them and creates an even bigger problem.
